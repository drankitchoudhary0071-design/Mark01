SCALPING 1m / 5m — first 6 months TRAIN / next 6 months OOS
Symbol: BTCUSDT (also probed PAXGUSDT 5m)
Period: 2025-08-07 → 2026-08-07  |  split at +182 days
Risk: 0.5%/trade

METHOD
------
- Build / select on TRAIN (first ~6m) only
- Freeze params, evaluate OOS (next ~6m)
- Require BOTH train and OOS: return>0, PF>1, ExpR>0, enough trades
- Variants tried: EMA+RSI, VWAP+RSI, BB+Stoch, MACD+EMA+Vol, ADX+EMA+RSI,
  MTF(15m/1h)+VWAP, Donchian breakout, high-vol VWAP+ADX+session filters
- Timeframes: 5m and 1m BTC; PAXG 5m probe
- Costs: taker ~0.30% RT, maker-ish ~0.09% RT, low ~0.05% RT, plus zero-cost diagnostic

VERDICT — PROFITABLE OOS (with costs)
--------------------------------------
NONE.

No 1m/5m scalp config was profitable on TRAIN and OOS together after
commission + spread + slippage. Scalp stop distances are too small vs friction.

Closest notes (NOT promoted — fail the train+OOS rule):
- BTC 5m high-vol VWAP sometimes OOS-green under maker with stop 4×ATR / TP 8×ATR
  but TRAIN was negative → luck / curve-fit, discarded.
- BTC 5m session 12–20 UTC, atr>0.1%, adx>15, stop3/tp6 under ~0.05% RT:
  TRAIN +2.5% PF1.20 — OOS −4.7% PF0.64 (fails OOS).
- Without costs, small edges exist (e.g. VWAP reclaim ~+4–9% train) but vanish
  once realistic fees are applied.
- PAXG 5m: same story — only zero-cost configs cleared train+OOS.

WHY
---
Median BTC 5m ATR% ≈ 0.13%. A 1.5–2×ATR stop is ~0.2–0.4% wide.
Round-trip friction 0.09–0.30% eats most/all of the R multiple. Need many
wins just to break even → expectancy goes negative.

RECOMMENDATION
--------------
Do not deploy 1m/5m scalp with taker fees on BTC/PAXG from this lab.
Use the higher-TF MTF Daily+4H RSI family (already OOS-profitable on BTC)
instead of forcing scalping.
